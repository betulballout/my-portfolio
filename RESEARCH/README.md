# Master's Thesis

## Monotone Iterative Techniques for Set-Valued Differential Equations in Metric Spaces

This research was completed as part of my Master of Science in Mathematics at Gebze Technical University.

The thesis focuses on monotone iterative techniques for solving set-valued differential equations in metric spaces.
